Absolutely! Here’s a **full, detailed IELTS Task 2 essay feedback** for your writing on the topic “Prevention is better than cure.”
For each issue, I’ll reference the original part and explain *why* it’s a problem, and suggest improvements (lexical, grammar, logic, coherence, and task achievement).

### 1. **Introduction**

**Original:**
According to some, in order to cure certain diseases or getting consult from doctors got exorbitant. So they support to find preventative methods that suit for budget. I firmly believe that treating illnesses are getting overpriced and should be avoided before it becomes harmful for others.

**Feedback:**

* **“in order to cure certain diseases or getting consult from doctors got exorbitant”**

  * *Issue*: This is grammatically incorrect and unclear.
  * *Upgrade*: Use: “the cost of curing certain diseases or consulting doctors has become exorbitant.”
  * *Why*: Past simple or present perfect needed, and “getting consult” is incorrect.
* **“So they support to find preventative methods that suit for budget.”**

  * *Issue*: “support to find” is awkward; “suit for budget” is incorrect collocation.
  * *Upgrade*: “So, they support investing in preventative methods that are more affordable.”
  * *Why*: Use the correct collocations (“investing in,” “affordable/preferable”).
* **“I firmly believe that treating illnesses are getting overpriced and should be avoided before it becomes harmful for others.”**

  * *Issue*: “Treating illnesses are” (subject-verb agreement); also, “should be avoided before it becomes harmful for others” is awkward and illogical.
  * *Upgrade*: “I firmly believe that the cost of treating illnesses is becoming too high, and investing in prevention is a better approach.”
  * *Why*: Subject-verb agreement and clarity.

### 2. **First Body Paragraph**

**Original:**
To commence with with, the reason of this is improved quality of life. Through taking heavy drugs, an infected organism can be promoted but demonstrates side effects to other parts. But the prevention keeps the overall health and well-being at the same level, allows being more productive. For example if someone has physical illness, indeed, being active contributes to improve recovering of human body, also releases the overwhelming on the the the brain and keeps away from the stress.

**Feedback:**

* **“To commence with with, the reason of this is improved quality of life.”**

  * *Issue*: Typo “with with”; “the reason of this is” is awkward.
  * *Upgrade*: “To begin with, prevention leads to an improved quality of life.”
  * *Why*: Use natural linking phrases and direct statements.
* **“Through taking heavy drugs, an infected organism can be promoted but demonstrates side effects to other parts.”**

  * *Issue*: “Infected organism can be promoted” is unclear, “demonstrates” not appropriate for “side effects.”
  * *Upgrade*: “Taking strong medication may treat the disease, but it often causes side effects in other parts of the body.”
  * *Why*: Use accurate biological and medical language.
* **“But the prevention keeps the overall health and well-being at the same level, allows being more productive.”**

  * *Issue*: Awkward, run-on sentence.
  * *Upgrade*: “In contrast, prevention helps maintain overall health and well-being, making individuals more productive.”
  * *Why*: Use contrast linking words and concise structure.
* **“For example if someone has physical illness, indeed, being active contributes to improve recovering of human body, also releases the overwhelming on the the the brain and keeps away from the stress.”**

  * *Issues*:

    * Awkward phrasing: “improve recovering of human body”
    * Typos: “the the the”
    * “releases the overwhelming on the brain” is unclear
  * *Upgrade*: “For example, if someone has a physical illness, staying active can aid recovery, reduce stress, and improve mental health.”
  * *Why*: More natural and precise.

### 3. **Second Body Paragraph**

**Original:**
Moreover, the prevention can help the durance of treatment. It means that staying at hospital and taking medicines spend much of time and energy because the line of injections, maybe, in profound amounts and a lot. At that time, the person who encountered contagious infection, they have a big chance to spread these diseases among clinics and community. As it can be seen two dangers have high occurence while treating in some spots. That's why the preference to prevenattive ways are more than long-term treatment.

**Feedback:**

* **“the prevention can help the durance of treatment.”**

  * *Issue*: “durance” is not used in this context, “the prevention” is awkward.
  * *Upgrade*: “Moreover, prevention can reduce the length and complexity of medical treatment.”
  * *Why*: Use common vocabulary.

* **“staying at hospital and taking medicines spend much of time and energy because the line of injections, maybe, in profound amounts and a lot.”**

  * *Issue*: Unclear meaning, awkward phrasing.
  * *Upgrade*: “Being hospitalized and undergoing treatments such as frequent injections is time-consuming and exhausting.”
  * *Why*: Clear, concise, natural English.

* **“At that time, the person who encountered contagious infection, they have a big chance to spread these diseases among clinics and community.”**

  * *Issue*: Awkward, run-on, “big chance” is informal.
  * *Upgrade*: “Furthermore, those with contagious diseases may spread infections to others in clinics and the wider community.”
  * *Why*: Natural and more formal.

* **“As it can be seen two dangers have high occurence while treating in some spots.”**

  * *Issue*: Unclear, “two dangers” not specified, “spots” is not the right word.
  * *Upgrade*: “Thus, there are two major risks associated with treatment: time and resource consumption, and the risk of spreading diseases.”
  * *Why*: Specify what the dangers are.

* **“That's why the preference to prevenattive ways are more than long-term treatment.”**

  * *Issue*: “preference to prevenattive ways are more than long-term treatment” is grammatically incorrect.
  * *Upgrade*: “For these reasons, preventative measures are preferable to long-term treatments.”
  * *Why*: Correct structure and clarity.

### 4. **Conclusion**

**Original:**
In conclusion, although the treatment gives a guarantee to the patient to be completely in good form, avoidance from the illness independently, saves the money, time and general health of human.

**Feedback:**

* **“treatment gives a guarantee to the patient to be completely in good form”**

  * *Issue*: Unnatural phrasing, overgeneralization (treatment doesn’t always guarantee full recovery).
  * *Upgrade*: “Although treatment can sometimes restore patients to full health…”
  * *Why*: Use cautious language.
* **“avoidance from the illness independently, saves the money, time and general health of human.”**

  * *Issue*: “avoidance from the illness independently” is awkward; “saves the money, time and general health of human” – missing article, plural.
  * *Upgrade*: “…preventing illness in the first place saves money, time, and protects people’s overall well-being.”
  * *Why*: Clear, natural, grammatically correct.

* **Grammar**: Multiple issues with subject-verb agreement, article use, and sentence structure.
